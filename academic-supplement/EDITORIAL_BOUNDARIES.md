## Preservation and normalization

The private raw files are retained byte-for-byte. This supplement does not edit them or publish them. Public prose corrects punctuation, removes profanity and identifying/private detail, and summarizes meaning without turning role-play into fact. The neutral description of the late injection is “planned scenario-framing change followed by simulation clarification.” It does not imply real families, contacts, or outcomes.

The public/private split excludes the street address embedded in recording titles; private biography, health, substance, fitness and physical-measurement details; identity variants; and unbounded offers of personal data.

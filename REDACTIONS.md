# Provenance and redaction limits

Aggregate sources are byte-hashed; every original STUDY/FAMILY artifact has an opaque SRC alias, kind, and supplied hash in `source_evidence.json`. The mapping is private. Stable hashed aliases exclude original work/native instance/request/receipt IDs, principals, private paths, identities, IPs, raw user/provider prompts, credentials, and hidden reasoning. Safe synthetic facts and answers remain. Hashes prove byte equality, not truth or custody.

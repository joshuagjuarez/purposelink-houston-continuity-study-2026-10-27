# Family continuity casebook

Append-only academic role-play. These 30 episodes are authored by the Memex-owned Astra operator from the preserved cases and Founder directions. Added circumstances are explicitly attributed branches, not newly discovered family facts. Provider replies are simulated; no real contact, capacity or outcome is established. Local executor evidence is recorded separately.

The reported study start is Tuesday, October 27, 2026, about 13:42 America/Chicago. Day/week/month/year labels are virtual case time. Observed artifact and tool times are separately recorded; nothing is backdated.

The fixed baseline remains 24 cells: 19 reconciled local responses, one local-model hold, four Maverick holds, and two strict-contract passes. This contract tests exact JSON/content/time/handoff behavior, not clinical competence or general usefulness. It has not been rerun or rescored.

The six source cases do not establish named families or household links. Their identifiers remain the continuity identifiers; no new family, cohort member or cross-case household relationship has been invented.

## What changes after a failed first plan

An unconfirmed ride becomes a transport dependency; a missed callback becomes a contact-design failure; a missing school record becomes a recovery task alongside enrollment; an inaccessible appointment remains provisional; a stale listing is withdrawn; and an unowned referral is assigned a return owner. None is silently converted into client noncompliance or service completion.

Case closure requires the person’s own account of need resolution and any consented confirming evidence. New loss of accommodation, unsafe contact, disrupted attendance, failed access, rejected documents, withdrawn support, unowned tasks or stale capacity claims reopen the relevant need.

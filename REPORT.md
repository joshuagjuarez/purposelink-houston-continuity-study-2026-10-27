# Academic audit report

## Question and relevance
This audit asks whether a frozen synthetic protocol preserved current facts, corrections, scope, and minimum-necessary handoffs. It responds narrowly to Yarneccia D. Dyson's October 22 article on simulation, ethical AI, workforce preparation, and human-centered community practice; the article did not evaluate or endorse Memex.

## Infrastructure, roles, and phases
Six physical hosts carried seven local placements: M1 GPU; Maverick CPU (also shared by Arthur, not an eighth host); MK1–MK3 CPU; and RTX GPU plus RTX CPU coordinator. Phones were excluded. `engines.json` separates configuration from decisive per-cell generation, external Root/Astra-ultra, Memex-owned Astra-high operators, and thirteen Sol-low engineering returns. The baseline began after a failed import startup; the same 24 IDs were retained. Protocol changes occurred before final refreeze. Publication and inventory are after cutoff.

## Results and continuity evidence
The case × condition × session table contains 24 cells: 19 returns, four shared-Maverick holds, one local hold, and two strict passes. Exact replay of the literally copied source scorer yields the same 19 score dictionaries—not mere artifact presence. It tests exact quoted content/source-session contracts, not semantic utility. `native_checks.json` preserves the actual 32-check curated record; all passed with zero model calls. A fresh client read the corrected canary on the SAME physical host, not an independent host.

## Family method and limitations
The full 30-episode casebook, offline filter timeline, conference, coordination map, and manual SOP cards preserve Astra-authored role-play lineages. Manual cards are not registered executables; no agency was contacted. Model/hardware are tied to cases; there is one trial per cell; limits differ (256/512); no randomization proof exists; protocol changed before refreeze; cache, capacity, interventions, and scheduling confound results. No clinical efficacy, real social outcome, causality, custody, or whole-fleet autonomy follows. Linux RAM is OS-visible MemTotal, not physical DIMM capacity. GPU listings do not claim use. Supplemental operator coverage has incomplete clocks and overlaps family returns.

The episode index is parsed from the six actual `PL-S01` through `PL-S06` case headings and the five headings Day 1, Week 1, Month 1, Month 3, and Year 1 beneath each case. The builder refuses any other count or order; it has no synthetic fallback. Thirty supplied JSONL continuity events are published alongside the index and checked for unique event identifiers. Their narrative developments, guards, reference identifiers, and action ownership are preserved as role-play evidence, not converted into service outcomes. The companion references file supports review of resource statements, but a public resource page does not demonstrate eligibility, capacity, referral acceptance, contact, or partnership.

Supplemental local returns are deduplicated by public execution alias. A returned answer can support an explicitly labeled inference of completion; it does not fill missing attempt metadata or establish general availability. Null attempt fields remain null for held or uncertain rows. Operator accounting reports original held reasons, return code, deadline/stop reason, elapsed time, recorded invocation count, and a lifecycle-deduplicated tracked count. Whole-call usage is shown without apportioning tokens to the study window. In particular, ASTRA-0017 remains held for `TOOL_CALL_CEILING_EXCEEDED`, and ASTRA-0023 remains uncertain after return code -15, `OPERATOR_DEADLINE`, and `NO_TERMINAL_TURN`. Earlier qualification outcomes are not retroactively improved by later records.

Exact engine settings and usage are available for supplemental rows where the supplied records provide them; fields not observed remain null or absent. Returned-answer completion is exposed only as a separate inference and does not change a null `model_called` flag.

## Reproducibility and proposals
`python3 verify_release.py .` recursively rejects changed/extra files, verifies the unchanged scorer hash, and recomputes all scores. Exact private-runtime reproduction is unavailable. Next: preregister randomized repeated trials, balance hardware/models, equalize output limits, freeze a semantic rubric in advance, test independent-host persistence, and use blinded review.

## Conditions, sessions, and result interpretation
The paired test crossed six synthetic cases, two continuity modes, and two sessions: 6 × 2 × 2 = 24 planned cells. Continuity mode was the manipulated variable. Session 1 and session 2 represented successive encounters; assignment remained fixed to the recorded node and model rather than randomized. Only PL-S04 session 1 passed the strict replayed scorer in each arm. Session 2 produced zero passes in both arms. Thus this scorer establishes no observed continuity benefit. The readable raw outputs include safe counterexamples: some answers were useful-looking but failed exact schema/content rules, illustrating why semantic usefulness and literal scoring must not be conflated.

The local-memory repair record is separate. Its 32 deterministic checks establish only the qualified operations and correction behavior tested on that host. It does not establish that personal biography was rebuilt, that another physical host independently retained state, or that every fleet placement had identical memory. A fresh client check was on the same physical host. These boundaries matter because process freshness is not host independence.

## Models, hardware, and responsibility
The human-readable inventories in [engines.json](engines.json) and [hardware.json](hardware.json) name six hosts—M1, Maverick, MK1, MK2, MK3, and RTX—and seven placements because RTX has GPU and CPU roles. Maverick's Arthur role shares that host and is not an eighth host. Recorded local generation involved Qwen placements and the RTX Nemo placement; configuration observations after cutoff do not retroactively prove generation. Three cloud support classes are separated: Memex-owned Sol-low engineering, Memex-owned Astra-high operation, and external Root Astra-ultra metadata. Known phase counts are reported without guessing a deduplicated grand total.

Responsibility is similarly bounded. The human Founder supplied intent and role-play injections. Root preserved sources and handled research, gateway mechanics, qualification tests, installs, launch, and verification. Memex operator/Astra owned operator activity; engineering/Sol produced publication engineering returns. Native Python performed deterministic assembly, hashing, and scorer replay. A model-written review is advisory evidence, never proof of a tool call or completed effect. No result supports describing these systems as proven autonomous social workers.

All recorded generation settings available in source evidence are retained per cell or placement: context, output cap, sampling profile, and token usage. The original task itself asked for JSON under 180 tokens, while engine caps of 256 or 512 were distinct runtime controls. Hardware, model, output, and context differences; scheduling, cache, and capacity; and technical interventions remain uncontrolled. Model-weight hashes, provider hardware, full root usage, and financial cost were not verified. The study is therefore an observational engineering study plus a 24-cell paired test, not a causal clinical trial.
